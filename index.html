    FAQs

    How long does it take?

    An Artist Room is live in a few days. An Artist World takes a week or two, and a Custom World is built with you over multiple weeks.

    Can I hear my own music in a room?

    Yes. Drag an MP3 onto any demo room, or tap + on its player, and the room plays it: lights, screens and the crowd move with your track. It only plays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

    What do I need to send?

    Your songs, your name, photos of anyone who'll be a character, artwork for posters, and the links you want the doors to open.

    Can I update songs for new releases?

    Yes. Song and poster updates are included with hosting, so your world keeps up with your releases.

    Does it work on phones?

    Yes. Rooms run in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, with touch controls. There's nothing to install.

    Where is it hosted?

    On Shaden Tech's hosting, with the first year included. After that it's $100 a year.
