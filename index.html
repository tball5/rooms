    FAQs

    How long does it take?

    A room built from one of ours is live in a few days. A world of connected rooms takes a week or two, and something new from scratch is built with you over multiple weeks.

    What do I need to send?

    Your songs, your name, photos of anyone who'll be a character, artwork for posters, and the links you want the doors to open.

    Can I update songs for new releases?

    Yes. Song and poster updates are included with hosting, so your world keeps up with your releases.

    Does it work on phones?

    Yes. Rooms run in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, with touch controls. There's nothing to install.

    Where is it hosted?

    On Shaden Tech's hosting, first year included.
